Booking & Account Policies

Slots and session length

Each slot is 15 minutes. Consecutive slots may be combined: 2 slots = 30 minutes, 4 slots = 60 minutes. The schedule shown in this preview runs from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM and includes 1:00 PM–2:00 PM.

Payment and confirmation

A booking must not be confirmed and credentials must not be issued until payment is verified by the payment provider on the server. A browser success message alone is not proof of payment.

Session access

Planned access window: 15 minutes before the scheduled start until the scheduled end. This session window is separate from the lifetime of a customer account and booking history.

Cancellation and refund

Proposed policy: eligible cancellations receive a refund of 75% of the session fee, excluding GST. The final cancellation deadline, processing time and applicable legal terms must be confirmed before launch and shown clearly at checkout.

Rescheduling

Proposed rule: rescheduling is allowed until 15 minutes before the session starts, subject to availability. Once the session is within 15 minutes of starting, rescheduling is disabled. Cancelled or moved slots should return to availability only after the server verifies that no other booking occupies them.

Astrologer accounts

Applications require admin approval. Admins can suspend or revoke access. An astrologer's private internal unique ID is visible only to authorised admin users; customers see the public display name. Public usernames use the ASTRO- prefix (for example, ASTRO-AkashTarot), while the immutable internal ID remains private and unchanged.

Privacy and security

Birth date, birth time, birth place and other personal details should be collected only when necessary, protected with access controls, and covered by a clear privacy notice. Passwords must be securely hashed; temporary credentials should be replaced on first login.

Important: these are proposed rules shown for design planning. This static preview does not implement payments, authentication, email, refunds, live availability or database access. Confirm the final terms and obtain appropriate professional advice before taking real bookings.
